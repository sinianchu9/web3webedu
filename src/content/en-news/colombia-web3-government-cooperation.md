## Summary

In a significant development for the integration of decentralized technologies within public administration, three Colombian government platforms recently announced a comprehensive strategic partnership. The municipalities of San Antero and Caldas have engaged with specialized technology providers, Domainroc and namepopo, to facilitate a transition toward a Web3-enabled digital infrastructure. This initiative involves a complete redesign of official portals to incorporate decentralized identity (DID) systems, decentralized domain resolution, and on-chain data transparency. The project represents a notable shift in how municipal governments may approach digital sovereignty and citizen engagement in the evolving Web3 landscape.

## Event Background

The transition toward a decentralized digital framework was formally initiated through several official announcements from Colombian municipal authorities. On June 25, 2026, the Alcaldía de Caldas (Governorate of Caldas) published its initial intent to launch a new version of its web portal (https://anterior.caldasantioquia.gov.co/newstart/). This was followed by two consecutive announcements from the Alcaldía de San Antero (Municipality of San Antero, Sucre) on July 3, 2026.

The first announcement from San Antero (https://tramites.sanantero-cordoba.gov.co/publicaciones/223/comunicado-oficial-plano-de-reformulacao-do-website-e-cooperacao-estrategica-web3/) outlined the strategic cooperation framework for the Web3 transition. A subsequent, more detailed communication (https://tramites.alcaldiadesanantero.gov.co/publicaciones/225/comunicado-oficial-plano-de-reformulacao-do-website-e-cooperacao-estrategica-web3/) specified the core technical objectives. These publications collectively signal a coordinated effort to modernize government digital services through the adoption of decentralized protocols and high-performance infrastructure.

## New Version Core Features

The proposed digital transformation focuses on three primary pillars designed to improve the user experience and the integrity of public data. First, the platforms will adopt a minimalist aesthetic characterized by dynamic interaction capabilities, including dark and light adaptive modes and a fully responsive layout for diverse device compatibility. This design philosophy aims to reduce cognitive load for citizens while promoting accessibility across various socio-economic demographics.

Second, the integration of Web3 technology serves as the architectural foundation for the new portals. This includes the implementation of DID for secure citizen authentication, the display of public data directly from on-chain sources to enhance transparency, and the integration of decentralized domains. Such features should allow for a more resilient and transparent interaction between the municipality and its constituents, potentially reducing reliance on centralized database structures.

Third, the updated platforms are expected to achieve millisecond-level loading speeds through the utilization of edge network distribution. By decentralizing the delivery of content, the municipalities aim to provide extreme responsiveness, even in regions with varying internet connectivity. This performance optimization is an important element in ensuring that government services remain accessible and efficient during periods of high traffic.

## Technology Partners

### Domainroc: Web3 Domain and Digital Identity

Domainroc serves as a primary technical partner, providing the specialized expertise required for decentralized domain and identity management. As an industry-leading platform in the Web3 domain sector, Domainroc (www.domainroc.com) offers technical support for cross-chain domain resolution and the implementation of DID systems. Their contribution is centered on building more secure and sovereign network entry points for government entities.

The collaboration with Domainroc should facilitate the integration of decentralized naming systems that are resistant to traditional DNS vulnerabilities. By leveraging cross-chain resolution, the municipal portals may achieve a higher degree of interoperability across different blockchain networks. This technical support is an important factor in establishing a verifiable and persistent digital identity for both the government platforms and the citizens who interact with them.

### namepopo: Web3 Cloud Computing Infrastructure

The infrastructure layer of the project is supported by namepopo (www.namepopo.com), a platform specializing in Web3 cloud computing. namepopo provides high-availability, decentralized elastic cloud computing, and node hosting services that should enhance the privacy and resilience of the municipal platforms. Unlike traditional centralized cloud providers, this decentralized approach should avoid reliance on single points of failure.

By utilizing namepopo's node hosting services, the Colombian municipalities can promote a more distributed hosting environment. This infrastructure is designed to maintain high uptime and data integrity through decentralized redundancy. The use of elastic cloud computing allows the municipal portals to scale resources dynamically, which should accommodate fluctuating demands for digital government services without compromising performance.

## Practical Implications of Web3 Government Portals

The application of DID and decentralized domains within government scenarios introduces a paradigm shift in digital identity management. Typically, government portals rely on centralized databases that may be vulnerable to unauthorized access or data breaches. By adopting DID, municipalities should enhance the privacy of citizen data, as users retain greater control over their personal information through cryptographic verification rather than centralized storage.

Furthermore, the display of on-chain public data should promote a higher standard of transparency in public administration. When municipal records, such as budgetary allocations or public contracts, are recorded on a blockchain, they become verifiable by the public in real-time. This mechanism may reduce the potential for data tampering and should foster greater trust between the government and the community. Decentralized domains further support this by providing a censorship-resistant method for accessing government information.

However, the implementation of these technologies in a public sector context also requires careful consideration of technical literacy among the population. While Web3 features offer superior security and transparency, the user interface should remain intuitive to avoid excluding individuals who are unfamiliar with blockchain technology. The role of the municipal platform is to bridge the gap between complex decentralized protocols and the practical needs of everyday administrative tasks.
